In one sentence
TrainingPlanner is a strength training planner for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch that
turns a plan into a workout you can follow, set by set, and helps your weights and reps go
up over time.

In one paragraph
TrainingPlanner plans strength training in cycles of any length, not just weeks. You lay a
cycle out on a board, train it with every set starting from what you lifted last time, and
when the cycle ends the next one is prescribed from what you logged: up where every rep
was done, repeated where reps were missed. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch,
kept in sync through the user's private iCloud. There is no account and no server behind
it: the App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected". The free app is a complete
training log for one repeating plan; TrainingPlanner Pro adds structured plans with phases
and deloads, automatic progression, periodization, readiness from Apple Health and an
on-device coach.
