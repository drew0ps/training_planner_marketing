Lean Build: get leaner without losing strength

When you're eating less to lose fat, the heavy lifts are what keep your muscle. Lean Build opens three days with one, then turns the rest of the session into circuits, and adds two days that are circuits from start to finish.

Who it's for

Lean Build is for an intermediate lifter in a fat-loss phase: eating less, wanting to keep the muscle and strength they've built, and happy to sweat. The diet does the fat loss; the training's job is to tell the body the muscle is still needed. Five days a week, 35 to 45 minutes each.

The workouts

The push, pull and leg days each open with one heavy lift for four sets of 6–8 (the bench press, the row, the squat) with full rest. Then a three-exercise circuit and a superset, 45 seconds between stations. The upper and lower circuit days are two circuits of three each, conditioning moves included.

How it progresses

By double progression, but keep your expectations honest: on less food, holding the heavy lifts where they are is a win. The app keeps asking for a rep more; take it when it's there, and don't worry when it isn't.

Start it in TrainingPlanner

  1. Open Programs, tap + and Start from a Template.
  2. Tap Template and pick Lean Build under Intermediate. The picture at the top shows its cycle, phases and deloads.
  3. Its cycle is a week: push, pull and legs on days 1 to 3, the upper and lower circuits on days 5 and 6. Change the training days if your week needs it, and tap Create.
  4. Start it from Today and pick your weights in the first week: the heavy lift something you could do for eight, the circuits a weight you can keep moving.

Tips

  • Protect the heavy lift. If a session has to be short, do the first exercise and skip a circuit, not the other way round.
  • Track your body. Body weight, waist and a photo every week or two tell you more than the scale alone; with Pro, the Body tab keeps them with trend lines that smooth out the day-to-day noise.
  • See what you eat beside what you train. If a food tracker saves to Apple Health, Today can show your calories and protein: turn on Show nutrition on Today in Settings.
