FinanceApp Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

The short version

FinanceApp doesn't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no bank connections, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party code that collects data.

What the app stores

The app stores what you enter, your assets and their values over time, income sources, credits, loans and mortgages, insurance policies with their coverage, and your settings, on your device only. It isn't synced to any server, and the developer has no access to it.

No connections

The app doesn't connect to banks or financial services and doesn't fetch prices or exchange rates. Every value in it is one you typed.

Exporting

When you export your data, the app makes the files on your device and hands them to the share sheet. Where they go from there is up to you.

Purchases

FinanceApp Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only checks with the App Store whether Pro is unlocked, and never sees your payment details.

Erasing

Settings › Erase All Data deletes everything the app stores on the device. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at https://trainingplanner.pro/financeapp/privacy, with a new effective date.
