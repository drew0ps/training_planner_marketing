Training methods

Supersets and circuits: more work in less time

While your chest rests, train your back. A superset alternates the sets of two exercises and rests once per round, so the same work fits in much less time. Add a third exercise and it's a circuit.

Not published yet · 3 min read

How a superset works

Two exercises, A1 and A2. Do a set of A1, then a set of A2, then rest; then A1 again, A2 again, rest; until the sets are done. Each muscle still gets its full rest between its own sets, because the other exercise is happening in between, but the clock only stops for one rest per round. Three sets each of two exercises take about the time of four or five straight sets.

What pairs well

  • Opposing muscles: chest and back, biceps and triceps, quads and hamstrings. One rests while the other works, and some lifters find they're a little stronger for it. The classic pairing.
  • Unrelated muscles: a press and a calf raise, a row and an ab exercise. Neither gets in the way of the other.
  • The same muscle (a compound set): an incline press then flyes. Not to save time, but to make a muscle work harder; it costs strength on the second exercise.

Avoid pairing two heavy lifts that tire the same thing, a squat and a deadlift, or two exercises at opposite ends of a busy gym.

Circuits

A circuit is a superset of three or more: A1, A2, A3, then a short break, then round again. With little rest between stations, the heart rate stays up, which makes circuits as much conditioning as strength work. They suit higher reps and moderate weights, on exercises you can move between quickly.

Set them up in TrainingPlanner

Supersets are made on the board, where you can see the whole session. Exercises in a superset share a coloured bar and a letter: A1 and A2, B1 and B2.

  1. Open the program's board.
  2. Tap an exercise's ⋯ menu and Superset with the exercise above or below it.
  3. To take one out, the same menu has Do on Its Own.
  4. In an exercise's page, Superset and Paired with do the same, with any exercise of the session.

In the workout, the app follows the superset: log a set of A1 and the next set is A2's, and the rest comes after the round.

Programs built on them

  • 10×10 Volume: ten sets of ten on two opposing lifts, done as a superset with short rests.
  • Golden Era Split: chest with back, shoulders with arms, paired for a lot of work in little time.
  • Conditioning Circuits and Lean Build: three-exercise circuits.
  • Most others pair their accessories: Push · Legs · Pull, Upper · Lower and more.
