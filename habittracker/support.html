Quicker answers

  • What the app does with your data (nothing it shouldn't): the privacy policy.
  • Pro: restore it in the app under Settings › Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Apple Watch: the watch shows what your iPhone last sent. If it looks out of date, open HabitTracker on your iPhone once, with the watch nearby.
  • Apple Health: link a habit to Health in its editor, then choose what the app may read in the Health app. HabitTracker only reads.
  • Your data: export it under Settings › Data. Erasing deletes it from the device and from iCloud.
  • What the app does and costs: the questions and answers on its page.

When you write

  • The app version, shown in Settings › About in the app.
  • Your iPhone and its iOS version, your Apple Watch and its watchOS version if it's about the watch, and what you tried and saw.
  • Screenshots help a lot, if it looks wrong.
