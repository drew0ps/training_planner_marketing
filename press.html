Texts to quote

Tagline

Train in phases. Progress every cycle.

In one sentence

TrainingPlanner is a strength training planner for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch that turns a plan into a workout you can follow, set by set, and helps your weights and reps go up over time.

In one paragraph

TrainingPlanner plans strength training in cycles of any length, not just weeks. You lay a cycle out on a board, train it with every set starting from what you lifted last time, and when the cycle ends the next one is prescribed from what you logged: up where every rep was done, repeated where reps were missed. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, kept in sync through the user's private iCloud. There is no account and no server behind it: the App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected". The free app is a complete training log for one repeating plan; TrainingPlanner Pro adds structured plans with phases and deloads, automatic progression, periodization, readiness from Apple Health and an on-device coach.

What sets it apart

  • Cycles, not weeks. A training cycle is as long as you train it: eight days, ten, or a classic week. Structured plans string cycles into phases with deloads.
  • The next cycle writes itself. Linear, double progression or RPE-based, for the whole program or per exercise. Every change is explained, and you accept, adjust or keep the plan as it was.
  • No account and no server. Training stays on the user's devices and in their private iCloud. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data.
  • The coach runs on the device. Coaching and program design use Apple Intelligence on supported devices; the plan and the log are never sent to a model elsewhere.
  • The watch can train alone. The Apple Watch app has today's workout and the next one, logs every set and records the workout, with the phone at home.
  • The free app is a complete log. One repeating plan with full logging, the rest timer, records, the calendar, the Apple Watch app and Apple Health, with no time limit.
