How it works

Say what you ate. Check the numbers. Watch the trend.

1

Log it your way

Type "2 eggs, toast and a coffee" or say it out loud. The meal is understood on your iPhone and every food comes back with its calories and macros.

  • Barcodes and nutrition labels are read on the device too.
  • Each proposed food says where its numbers come from: the built-in library, Open Food Facts, USDA, or an estimate.
  • Nothing is saved until you've checked it. Swap a food, change an amount, then confirm.
2

Keep your day in view

Today shows what's left of your calories, protein, carbs and fat, your water, and each meal with what you planned beside what you ate. Set your own targets, or let the app suggest them from your goal.

  • Fiber, sodium, caffeine and twenty vitamins and minerals, if you want to see them.
  • A fasting timer, and your food library a tap away when logging: favorites, recent and custom foods, saved meals and recipes.
  • Home Screen widgets: your day as a watch face, the calories round the bezel and the macros in three dials, or the numbers you pick.
3

See your progress take shape

Weight, body fat and lean mass over time, with body-composition estimates that are labeled as estimates. Every week, Insights sums up your averages, your adherence and the trend, and the weekly coach leaves you a personal note.

  • Weight and body fat import from Apple Health, if you opt in.
  • Reports you can read in a minute, not a dashboard to study.
  • Smart suggestions: foods that fit what's left of your calories and protein today.

Privacy

Your meals are nobody's business.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only network requests are the food and barcode lookups you trigger.

  • Kept on your iPhoneand in your private iCloud, which only you can read. Nothing goes to a server of ours, because there isn't one.
  • AI on the deviceVoice and sentences are understood by Apple's on-device models. Only individual food names and barcodes are looked up online.
  • Apple Health, if you want itEnergy, macros and micronutrients go to Health; weight and body fat come in. Every part is optional, and nothing read from Health is synced or sent anywhere.
  • Yours to deleteSettings › Data › Delete All Data leaves nothing behind: no entries, no widget numbers, no reminders.

Questions

Before you download

Is DietApp Pro free?

Yes. The free app tracks calories, macros, water and fasting with the built-in library, Open Food Facts and USDA, with 3 AI logs a week and 3 saved meals to try, and no time limit. Pro removes the limits and adds label scanning, meal plans, the weekly coach and the rest of the Pro list above.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later. The on-device understanding of what you type or say uses Apple Intelligence, so it needs an iPhone that supports it; without it you can still log everything by typing, searching or scanning.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, ever. Your food log and measurements are kept on your iPhone and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database. The built-in library works offline; Open Food Facts and USDA lookups need a connection, and fail gracefully without one.

How accurate are the numbers?

Library, Open Food Facts and USDA foods carry their source's figures. When the on-device model has to estimate a food, it says so, and you can correct it before saving. Calorie, macro and body-composition figures are estimates, as they are in every tracker.

I follow a plan from a coach or dietitian. Does that work?

Yes. Build the plan once, or open the one your coach shares as a file. Each day's planned foods appear beside what you actually ate, so you log the plan with one tap or see where you went off it. Plans can have alternatives for each food and can log themselves daily.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your App Store subscription settings, at any time. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and your log is always kept.
