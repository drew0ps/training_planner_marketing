HabitTracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

The short version

HabitTracker doesn't collect your data. Your habits, checks, routines, journal and settings stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party code that collects data.

What the app stores

The app stores your habits, what you logged each day, your routines, journal entries, categories, life modes, experiments and settings on your device. The developer has no access to it.

iCloud sync

When you're signed in to iCloud, the app syncs this data through your private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit) to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. It is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, not on servers the developer controls, and the developer can't read it. Without iCloud, everything stays on the device.

Apple Health

If you link a habit to Apple Health, the app reads only the data that habit needs (for example steps, exercise minutes, mindful minutes or sleep) to check it off for you. It reads, never writes, and only after you allow it in the Health permissions. Health data is used on your device and is not sent anywhere, used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app gets today's habits from your iPhone and sends back what you check off, directly between your own devices.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your device, only for the habits you set a reminder for. Widgets read today's habits from a space on your device that only the app and its widgets share.

Purchases

HabitTracker Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only checks with the App Store whether Pro is unlocked, and never sees your payment details.

Exporting

When you export your data, the app makes a file and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at https://trainingplanner.pro/habittracker/privacy, with a new effective date.
