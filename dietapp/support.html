Quicker answers

  • What the app does with your data (nothing it shouldn't): the privacy policy.
  • Purchases: restore them in the app under Settings › Subscription; with Pro active, Manage Subscription is in the same place.
  • Food lookups: turn either database off under Settings › Food Data Sources; the app then looks up foods only in its on-device catalog.
  • Your data: export it as CSV or JSON, or delete everything, under Settings › Data. Deleting the app removes it from that device only; data in iCloud stays until you delete it in the app or in your iCloud settings.
  • Apple Health: switch it on or off under Settings › Apple Health, and choose exactly what is shared in the Health app.
  • Apple Watch: the watch shows what your iPhone last sent. If it looks out of date, open DietApp Pro on your iPhone once, with the watch nearby.
  • What the app does and costs, and what it needs: the questions and answers on its page.

When you write

  • The app version, shown in Settings › About in the app.
  • Your iPhone and its iOS version, your Apple Watch and its watchOS version if it's about the watch, and what you tried and saw.
  • Screenshots help a lot, if it looks wrong.
