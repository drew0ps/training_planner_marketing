Bench Specialization: eight weeks for a bigger bench press

Bench three times a week: heavy, paused for volume, and close-grip. Eight weeks, a volume block then an intensity block, and everything else kept ticking over on one day. A stuck bench press has nowhere to hide.

Who it's for

Bench Specialization is for an intermediate lifter whose bench press has stalled, or who wants it to lead for a couple of months: before a meet, a test, or just a number they've been chasing. Four days a week, about an hour each. Afterwards, go back to a balanced program; the bench keeps most of what it gained.

The workouts

The heavy day is a top set of the bench press and back-offs about 10 % lighter, with rows and some shoulder and triceps work. The volume day pauses every rep on the chest for a second, then incline presses and weighted dips. The close-grip day presses with the hands at shoulder width, elbows tucked, for the triceps and the lockout. One day covers legs and back.

Week by week

The first block builds volume on the sets above. In the second, the intensity block, the reps come down and the weights go up for three weeks before the last deload:

How it progresses

By double progression within each block: the top set climbs from three reps to five, the back-offs from five to seven, then each steps up. In the intensity block the ranges are lower, so the weights jump: start its first week from where the volume block left off, a little heavier for fewer reps.

Twenty-one work sets a week for the chest and fifteen for the triceps: the bench's prime movers. The back gets thirteen, to keep the shoulders healthy under all that pressing; the legs get enough to hold their strength.

Tips

  • Pause properly. A one-second pause on the chest, every rep, with the bar still: it's what makes the volume day work.
  • Look after the shoulders. Face pulls and rear-delt work are in the plan for a reason; don't skip them to bench more.
  • Test at the end, if you like: after the last deload, work up to a heavy single. The app marks a new best with a trophy as you log it.
