Glutes & Legs Focus: two lower-body days that put the glutes first

Hip thrusts and Romanian deadlifts one day, squats and lunges the other, and two shorter upper-body days between them. The glutes get more work than anything else, from every angle, and the rest of the body keeps up.

Who it's for

Glutes & Legs Focus is for an intermediate lifter who wants stronger, bigger glutes and legs above all, and a balanced upper body without the same priority. Four days a week: two lower-body days of about an hour, two upper-body days of about 45 minutes.

The workouts

The hinge day is built on the hip thrust, with a pause at the top, and the Romanian deadlift, then split squats leaning forward a little, hip abduction, leg curls and cable kickbacks. The squat day is built on the back squat and the leg press with the feet high and wide, then lunges, a one-sided hip thrust and leg extensions. The upper days are compact: a press, a pull, a superset or two.

How it progresses

By double progression, each set in its range. The hip thrust takes big jumps well, 5 kg at a time; the split squats and kickbacks climb in small ones.

Phases and deloads

Two phases of five weeks, Accumulation and Intensification, each ending with a deload week.

Twenty-five work sets a week for the glutes, as much as any program in the app but Kettlebell Full Body, and sixteen for the quads. The upper body gets four to nine sets per muscle: enough to keep growing, not to lead.

Tips

  • Pause at the top of the hip thrust. A second with the hips fully extended is where the glutes do the most.
  • Feel it where it should be. The exercise pages show the muscles each one trains; if the lower back takes over on the Romanian deadlift, lighten it.
  • Measure the result. With Pro, a thigh measurement in the Body tab (add your own, like the hips) and a photo show the change over the program.
