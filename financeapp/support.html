Quicker answers

  • What the app does with your data (nothing it shouldn't): the privacy policy.
  • Pro: restore it in the app under Settings › Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Your data is on this iPhone only. Export a backup under Settings › Data Export (Pro) before you change phones, or restore your iPhone's own backup on the new one.
  • Erase everything under Settings › Erase All Data; deleting the app does the same.
  • What the app does and costs: the questions and answers on its page.

When you write

  • The app version, shown in Settings › About in the app.
  • Your iPhone and its iOS version, and what you tried and saw.
  • Screenshots help a lot, if it looks wrong.
