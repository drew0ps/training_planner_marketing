Golden Era Split: chest with back, shoulders with arms, legs, twice a week

Chest and back together, in supersets. Shoulders and arms the next day, legs the day after, then all of it again. The six-day split of the classic bodybuilding gyms: huge volume, every muscle twice a week, and sessions that never stand still.

Who it's for

The Golden Era Split is for an advanced lifter after maximum muscle, who can train six days a week and loves volume. It's the most demanding program in TrainingPlanner by weekly sets. Sessions are 50 to 70 minutes, kept to that by supersets.

The workouts

The chest and back day pairs a press with a pull three times: bench press with wide-grip pull-ups, incline presses with rows, flyes with pullovers. The shoulders and arms day pairs lateral raises with rear-delt raises and curls with triceps work. Legs are straight sets, squats first. Drop sets finish the flyes and the triceps extensions.

How it progresses

By double progression: reps to the top of each range, then weight. Pull-ups climb by reps. The drop sets start about 20 % below the set before and follow it.

Phases and deloads

Two phases of five weeks, Accumulation and Intensification, each ending with a deload week. At this volume, the deloads are part of the program, not a break from it.

Over thirty work sets a week for the chest and over twenty for the shoulders and back. That's beyond the top of the usual weekly ranges, and it's the point of the program: a short block of very high volume for someone who recovers from it. If you don't, the three questions after each exercise will say so, and the next cycle takes sets away.

Start it in TrainingPlanner

  1. Open Programs, tap + and Start from a Template.
  2. Tap Template and pick Golden Era Split under Advanced. The picture at the top shows its cycle, phases and deloads.
  3. Its cycle is a week: chest and back, shoulders and arms, and legs on days 1 to 3, and again on days 4 to 6. Change the training days if your week needs it, and tap Create.
  4. Start it from Today and pick your weights in the first three sessions; the second half of the week starts from what you did in the first.

Tips

  • Claim two stations. A superset needs both exercises ready; in a busy gym, the bench and the pull-up bar near each other.
  • Don't add to it. The volume is already high; extra sets on top cost more recovery than they give.
  • Eat for it. Six days of this on too little food stops working fast.
