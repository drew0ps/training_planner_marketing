Dumbbell Full Body: three workouts with dumbbells and a bench

A pair of adjustable dumbbells and a bench are enough for a complete program: squats, presses, rows, hinges, and the small stuff, in three workouts that rotate through the week. At home, or in a gym where the racks are always taken.

Who it's for

Dumbbell Full Body is for a beginner who trains at home with adjustable dumbbells and a bench, or in a gym where dumbbells are what's free. It builds muscle and strength on every major movement: a squat or lunge, a press, a row, a hinge, every workout. Sessions take about 45 minutes.

How it progresses

Dumbbells go up in steps of 2 kg or so, which is a big jump on a press: too big to make every workout. So every exercise climbs by double progression: reps at the same weight until every set is at the top of its range, then the next pair, back to the bottom. In the first workout, pick dumbbells you could lift two or three more times than the bottom of the range.

If your dumbbells are in pounds while the app is in kilograms (or the other way round), set the exercise's weights to its own unit on its page: the steps then match your rack.

Squats, split squats, step-ups, hip thrusts and Romanian deadlifts make the glutes the most-worked muscle. Back, chest and shoulders get a press or a row every workout; the arms a short superset in each.

Start it in TrainingPlanner

  1. Open Programs, tap + and Start from a Template.
  2. Tap Template and pick Dumbbell Full Body under Beginner. The picture at the top shows its cycle, phases and deloads.
  3. Its cycle is a week: Dumbbell A, B and C on days 1, 3 and 5. Change the training days if your week needs it, and tap Create.
  4. Start it from Today and pick your dumbbells in the first workouts: a pair you could lift two or three more times than the bottom of each range.

On iPad and Mac

The same program with more of it on screen: the iPad and the Mac lay the board out day by day, side by side, and your programs and workouts sync between them through your private iCloud.

Free or Pro. Dumbbell Full Body is a beginner template, so the free app runs it as a repeating plan: in Programs, tap + › Build My Own › Repeating Plan and start from the Dumbbell Full Body cycle. The weights then start from what you lifted last time, and you add the step yourself. The structured program, with its phases, its deloads and the progression done for you, is part of TrainingPlanner Pro.

Tips

  • Tell the app what you have. In Settings › Equipment, a home gym with dumbbells and a bench: suggestions and swaps then fit it.
  • Slow down at the top of the range. When your dumbbells top out, slower reps and a pause at the stretch make a light weight hard again.
  • Outgrown your dumbbells? Move to Two-Day Full Body or Upper · Lower in a gym.
