Machine Full Body: first steps in the gym

Seven machines and cables, the same session three times a week. No barbell to balance and nothing to learn but a seat height, so you can push close to failure from week one and watch the numbers climb.

Who it's for

Machine Full Body is for a first few months in a gym: someone who wants to build muscle and confidence without learning the barbell lifts first. Machines guide the movement, so the work is in the muscles rather than the balance, and a set taken close to failure is safe. It's also a good program to come back on after an injury or a long break. The session takes about an hour.

How a cycle looks

One session, three times a week, with a day between: days 1, 3 and 5 of a seven-day cycle. Pick any three days that aren't in a row.

The workout

Legs, chest, back twice, shoulders, hamstrings and abs, in that order: the big movements first while you're fresh, the small ones last.

How it progresses

Every exercise moves by double progression: add reps at the same weight until every set reaches the top of its range, then the next weight on the stack, and back to the bottom. In the first session, pick weights you could do about three more reps with, as the note on the leg press says. From the second session on, the app sets the weight and the reps to aim for.

Phases and deloads

Two phases of six weeks, Base and Build, each ending with a deload week.

The back gets two exercises, which is a deliberate counterweight to a day spent sitting forward. Arms get no work of their own; the presses, the pulldown and the row train them along the way. Add a curl and a pressdown at the end if you'd like more.

Tips

  • Set the machine up the same way every time. Note the seat height and pad positions in the exercise's notes; a different setup is a different exercise.
  • Machines differ between gyms. If you train somewhere else, the numbers won't carry over; that's fine, the progression starts again from what you log there.
  • Push the last set. The last two or three reps of each set are where the growth is. A machine makes them safe to chase.
  • Next: once the routine feels easy to run, move to free weights with Two-Day Full Body or Full Body A/B.
