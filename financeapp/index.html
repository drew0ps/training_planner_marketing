Questions

Before you download

Is FinanceApp Pro free?

Yes, to start. The free app keeps up to 10 entries in every area, with the dashboard, the coverage search and the last three months of your net worth. Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited entries, the full history, export and every theme.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, on purpose. FinanceApp Pro has no bank connection and fetches no prices: every number is one you typed, and nothing leaves your iPhone.

Is my data backed up?

Your inventory is stored on your iPhone only, so it's part of your iPhone's own backup. With Pro, export a complete JSON backup whenever you like.

Is it a budgeting app?

No. It's an inventory: what you own, earn, owe and are covered for, and your net worth over time. There are no transactions to import or envelopes to fill.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or later.
